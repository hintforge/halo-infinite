### How the variants play (web research 2026-10-09)

_source: web research 2026-10-09: per-variant Halopedia pages (https://www.halopedia.org/<Variant_Name>, read through a summarising fetch tool); Kotaku, "What Halo Infinite's Weapon Variants Actually Do" (https://kotaku.com/what-halo-infinite-s-weapon-variants-actually-do-1848362095, Dec 2021, one author's opinions); r/halo "Thoughts on the campaign weapon sandbox?" (https://www.reddit.com/r/halo/comments/rxqkbp/) · capture: web_fetch. Practitioner opinion on the campaign variants is thin: Kotaku is the only multi-weapon campaign review found; no campaign opinions were found for the Valor variants; a large Reddit variant guide was multiplayer-only and dropped. Numbers left out (Halopedia mixes modes)._

[spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium (wiki mechanism) / low (Kotaku opinion, single source) · class:community-wiki + editorial-en]

- **Rapidfire Pulse Carbine:** clearly faster fire than the base; the wiki contradicts itself on per-bolt damage. Kotaku: makes a weak gun "slightly less useless".
- **Arcane Sentinel Beam:** semi-automatic (one shot per pull), far more damage per ammo, much smaller battery. Kotaku: "you can skip this one" (calls it only a bit stronger, which conflicts with the wiki's read).
- **Duelist Energy Sword:** faster movement and higher jumps while held, not sprint speed.
- **Calcine Disruptor:** harder-hitting, delayed blast catches neighbours; small magazine, frequent reloads; delay suits still or bunched targets. Kotaku: "the world's worst aim" still lands shocks.
- **Unbound Plasma Pistol:** stronger, faster bolts; full overcharge releases a tracking spread; does not stall vehicles (Kotaku agrees).
- **Stalker Rifle Ultra:** faster fire, less damage per bolt, more shots before overheat, much weaker scope; Kotaku: better for close quarters. Resolves the "faster bullets" line in the HVT table toward the in-game text.
- **M41 Tracker:** locks on to vehicles and infantry; the base SPNKr has no lock-on in this game. Kotaku: "should be illegal".
- **Riven Mangler:** three-shard spread per pull, slower fire. Kotaku: "a bona fide shotgun up close".
- **Rushdown Hammer:** more reach, faster movement and jumps, run speed unchanged; Kotaku likens it to the classic launch-yourself hammer.
- **Volatile Skewer:** detonates at once on an enemy or vehicle, late on a missed surface; shorter effective range than the base.
- **Ravager Rebound:** shots bounce at least once before detonating. Kotaku calls it the worst variant.
- **Pinpoint Needler:** better tracking, tighter spread, fewer needles to supercombine, no zoom.
- **Purging Shock Rifle:** longer bursts, keeps the scope, one hit stuns a vehicle. Kotaku: "the use case isn't great" solo.
- **Backdraft Cindershot:** shots split and each piece bursts on impact; no zoom, so no mid-flight steering.
- **Scatterbound Heatwave:** faster shots that home after a ricochet; bounce off a surface near the target. Kotaku: it doesn't waste scarce ammo.
- **Valor variants (wiki only):** Striker Sidekick hits harder with more recoil; Longshot is more accurate and harder-hitting with a smaller magazine and no zoom; Convergence Bulldog has a bigger magazine and tighter spread; Impact Commando is full-auto with more spread and less zoom; BR75 Breacher hits harder and faster with a smaller magazine and weaker zoom (close vs mid range disputed); S7 Flexfire trades damage per shot for a large magazine and a forgiving reticle at mid range; Pursuit Hydra locks faster and farther, rocket speed disputed (in-game text faster, wiki slower). Reported from play 2026-10-13: the S7 Flexfire Sniper picked up on Pelican Down (grid D6) shows a 10-round magazine on the HUD, with no real zoom or only a weak two-level zoom (play report; base-sniper capacity and zoom not checked here).
- **Base tips:** Skewer kills light and air vehicles in one or two shots and shoves heavy ones; Cindershot can be steered while zoomed and its blast pulls enemies in; two Shock Rifle hits stall drivable vehicles.

- Boss (HVT) weapons spawn at any FOB rack after the target dies; Valor weapons are requisitioned at any FOB once you have the Valor. Halopedia: "A selection of these weapons can also be found in various areas of the campaign's open world" -- the page names no spots (MapGenie spots are in `../optional_zones/*_collectibles.md` "Unique Weapon" rows). [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · class:community-wiki]

## Scorpion Gun (exploit, overworld pickup)

A detached Scorpion tank cannon sits on a chunk of Infinity hull northeast of Outpost Tremonius (a fast-travel node, not a lettered FOB), reached with the Grappleshot. It was removed in the Season 2 update (2022-05-03) but saves that already had it kept it; the 2022-05-25 update restored the pickup for everyone ("The 'tank gun' glitch in the Outpost Tremonius mission has been restored" -- official patch notes). [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · confidence: high · Confirmed: 4 sources]

- **Legendary-run value:** the Scorpion Gun knocks Escharum back with every shot during his House of Reckoning fight -- see `../npcs/escharum.md`. [spoiler: late-game · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 0 · entity: escharum · entity-status: hostile]

## Field weapon finds, carry limits, and FOB storage (web research 2026-09-28)

_source: web research 2026-09-28 (gamepressure.com combat/weapons page; GameRevolution, Twinfinite, GamesRadar FOB guides via search summaries; Halopedia "Legendary variant weapons")_

- **You carry 2 standard weapons plus 1 heavy at a time.** Gamepressure: "you cannot store all weapons you find in the game world in the hero's inventory." No source found describes any way to bank a field weapon at a FOB or mark a weapon cache on the map. This is an absence claim from a small search, not a proof. [spoiler: none · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Single source (gamepressure) plus absence in 3 other guides -- verify · class:editorial-en]
- **What replaces storage is the FOB rack:** every weapon you have unlocked (Valor for UNSC, HVT kills for Banished variants) can be spawned again at any captured FOB, free and unlimited, and stays unlocked permanently. Gamepressure adds that field weapons sit on racks or drop from enemies and "most can be found anytime." [spoiler: none · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: high (rack behavior, matches `mechanics.md`) / medium (field-rack availability, single source)]
- **Open-world variant finds:** Halopedia says some variant weapons sit in open-world spots "independent of FOBs or HVT locations," and that boss-tier weapons can be re-spawned at a FOB once their target is dead. Halopedia names neither the spots nor which weapons, so which field caches hold a weapon you cannot get from a rack is **not confirmed**. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: low · Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]

- **Backdraft Cindershot found in a field cache (play report 2026-09-28):** the guides found (AlcastHQ, Halopedia, GamesRadar, Windows Central, Pro Game Guides via search) describe the rack unlock only as the HVT kill; none says whether picking one up in the field also unlocks it at FOB racks. ## Shock Rifle -- how it works (web research 2026-09-29)

_source: web research 2026-09-29 (Halopedia "Shock Rifle" fetched; Gamepur and TweakTown via search summary)_

- **Two fire modes:** the primary trigger fires a burst that builds a chain EMP shock on the target; the alternate fire takes a short charge and releases one EMP bolt. [spoiler: none · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 2 sources (Halopedia fetched; Gamepur summary) · class:community-wiki]
- **Best use is against vehicles:** roughly two hits disable most vehicles for a few seconds; manned turrets keep firing. Against infantry, electricity arcs from the target to enemies bunched near it, but the arc damage is small. [spoiler: none · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 2 sources · class:community-wiki]
- **Campaign stats:** 20-bolt magazine, 5-bolt bursts (Halopedia). No campaign damage or kill numbers were found; do not fill the gap with multiplayer figures. [spoiler: none · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]
- Enemy types that carry it were omitted here on purpose (enemy dial at 0).
