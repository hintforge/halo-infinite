## Equipment-focused (most common guide recommendation)

Put your first Spartan Cores into the Grappleshot (Voltaic, then Quick Shot) and the Shield Core (Fortress, Bastille) before anything else. Multiple sources converge on this exact order, including a German-language guide. [Confirmed: 4 sources, 2 languages]

## Weapon-focused

Pair a precision rifle (BR75 or Stalker Rifle Ultra) with a close-range or anti-shield option (Duelist Energy Sword, Shock Rifle). One Steam LASO guide recommends a Sniper + Energy Sword loadout in the open world and the Arcane Sentinel Beam for linear missions. [Single source, verify · class:forum]

## Hybrid

Thruster for dodging Escharum's hammer phase, or Drop Wall to recover shields before grappling out (a stray hit mid-grapple resets shield recharge). [Single source, verify · class:editorial-en]

## Where guides disagree

DiamondLobby calls Threat Sensor's last tier "definitely not worth it" (9 cores just to see enemy health), but LASO (Legendary, All Skulls On) guides rely on constant Threat Sensor coverage for the Jega 'Rdomnai and Escharum fights. [Contradicted across sources · entity: jega_rdomnai, escharum]
