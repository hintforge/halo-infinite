**Famine access (web research 2026-10-14):** the island (K9, east of H7) is at the far east edge of the world map and Pure Xbox says a WASP or Banshee is needed to reach it; it names no launch point. Method (Gamepur, Sportskeeda, TrueAchievements forum, all read in full): call a Wasp from a FOB in the area if unlocked, otherwise grapple a Banshee as it flies close to hijack it, then fly east to the island. A forum player reports using the Banshees that hang around FOB November (H7); a Banshee also waits on FOB Hotel's east cliff (southwest, far from here). Twinfinite alone says grappling across on foot is "not impossible" and gives no route; no other source describes one, so an on-foot route is unproven. Gamepur's prerequisite is the mission that opens this region. On the island the Skull lies on the north side beside a dead golden-armoured Elite; one source adds a Pulse Carbine nearby. ## Rules and exceptions

- **Continue vs. Load (older builds):** a Steam-forum LASO report said choosing "Continue" from the main menu turns Skulls off; the Winter Update (Nov 2022) notes say previously enabled Skulls no longer randomly disable when you resume via Continue, so this should not apply on the current build (whether the report predates the fix is unverified). [Single source, verify · class:forum · spoiler: none · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · achievement: Headmaster]
- **Bandana collection display bug:** fixed by the Winter Update -- Bandana previously didn't show as collected on the TacMap. [Confirmed: 1 official source]
- **Achievements:** Headstrong (first Skull) and Catacomb (all 12) -- see `../achievements.md`. Headmaster additionally requires all 12 Skulls turned ON during a full Legendary run.
