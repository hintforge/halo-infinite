## Max-equipment achievements

Max one piece of equipment (9 cores each) for each of five achievements; the mapping was resolved at P3: **Thrusters On Full = Thruster; Impervious = Drop Wall; Big Brother = Threat Sensor; Getting Defensive = Shield Core; Reaching Out = Grappleshot**. With 45 cores all five are earnable on one save; in co-op each player needs their own 9 per piece. Full entry and sourcing (the Thruster pairing is by elimination, medium confidence): `../achievements.md`. [spoiler: none · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: high (four pairings) / medium (Thruster) · achievement: Thrusters On Full, Impervious, Big Brother, Getting Defensive, Reaching Out]

## Why upgrade order matters

Several collectibles and HVTs (e.g. the Fog and Thunderstorm Skull perches) sit on sheer hexagonal pillars that are difficult to reach reliably without Grappleshot's Quick Shot (rank 3) -- see `../mechanics.md` "Equipment."
