## Hint ladder

**Lvl 1 (nudge):** The doors near the console changed color for a reason -- go see what opened up.

**Lvl 2 (more):** After using the console reached by a hardlight bridge, the side doors turn from red to blue. One of the newly-blue doors holds something you need to carry across the room.

**Lvl 3 (step-by-step):**
1. Cross the hardlight bridge and use the console (triggers the Escharum/Echo 216 cutscene).
2. The side doors switch from red to blue. Grapple to the right-hand door and pick up the Power Seed.
3. Throw and grapple the Power Seed across the gaps to the opposite room; slot it into the receptacle in the back-right corner. If it falls, it respawns -- no fail state.
4. Later, in the symmetrical gold-walled room, grapple up through the high window (only open because the seed was placed). The Skull is guarded by 4 stealth Elites -- clear them or grab-and-run.

A French-language guide corroborates this exact sequence, naming the parts "Noyau d'energie" (Power Seed) and "Creuset" (receptacle). [translated from: French · confidence: high · Confirmed: 5 sources, 2 languages]
