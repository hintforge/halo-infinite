## Achievements available here

- **Unearthed** -- shut down the mining laser (also gates the story-edge to Conservatory, see `../nav/architecture.md`). **Workplace Safety Violation** -- kill an enemy with that laser. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · achievement: Unearthed, Workplace Safety Violation]
- **You, Me, Same Page** (co-op) -- destroy all cooling towers at the "Dig Site" within 60 seconds of each other. P2 places those cooling towers at this mission's Banished dig site (official Winter Update achievement list), resolving P1's open question about which area "Dig Site" means. P3 adds a second source: TrueAchievements groups it with the main-story achievements and notes it is the only one there that cannot be done solo, and the Waypoint co-op preview calls the Banished dig site the largest single objective the tether lets a team split across. - Tactics (P3): he is a Brute Chieftain with a Gravity Hammer who charges with no stealth. Jump as the hammer lands, grapple away to keep your distance, strip his shield with plasma weapons, then switch to kinetic. Shock weapons and Dynamo grenades stun him, and his straight-line charges make Plasma and Spike grenades easy sticks. Explosive tanks sit behind the door on the right at the start, and three more are behind the furthest right door on the upper level.
