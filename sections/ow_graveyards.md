## Boss

**Brothers Grim** (hidden) -- defeat Hyperius and Tovarus. The trigger itself is `progression`; the achievement's name is `story`-tier, since it implies the pair are brothers -- a reveal the bare trigger doesn't carry. Tovarus wields a Scrap Cannon (see `../items/weapons.md`). P3 promoted the pair to one combined entity file: `../npcs/tovarus_hyperius.md`. [spoiler: progression (trigger) / story (name/relationship) · enemy-tier: 1 / 3 · puzzle-tier: 0 / 3 · entity: tovarus_hyperius · entity-status: hostile · achievement: Brothers Grim [S]]

- The fight (P3): the only boss fight with no intro cutscene. It is an open-world fight among Grunts and low Brutes in the wreck-strewn marsh. Hyperius drives an armored gold Brute Chopper and fights on foot with a Ravager once it is destroyed; Tovarus leaps between platforms and the broken bridge with a Scrap Cannon. - Kill order is [Contradicted across sources]: five guides (Windows Central, ScreenRant, GamerTweak, The Nerd Stash, Samurai Gamers) say kill Hyperius first -- lure him to the west side, skewer or rocket the Chopper, and drain his shield with a Pulse Carbine or Sentinel Beam. ## Collectibles

5 Spartan Cores, 1 locker (holds the Warmaster's Prize BR coating -- single source, GamerGuides), 3 Banished logs, 5 Spartan logs, 4 propaganda towers. UNSC and Spartan logs are bundled together in the maps and menus but counted separately for achievements, which is why some guides call the five here "UNSC logs" and others "Spartan logs". [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · confidence: high · Confirmed: 4 sources, 1 language]

**Postgame access (P3):** [Contradicted across sources]. Neoseeker, Gamerpillar and Halopedia treat the region as mission-only; Gamepressure ("none of them are missable"), Digital Trends and a Microsoft statement relayed by Kotaku ("After you finish campaign missions, you can freely explore most of the map") point the other way, and the Winter Update removed an erroneous kill volume near the eastern AA gun. One Steam user confirms flying a Banshee off the island toward Reformation after the mission; no first-hand postgame walk-in report was found, and two Steam users report kill barriers around the island. Treat the five cores as **reachable via Mission Replay of Pelican Down (guaranteed); free-roam re-entry likely but unverified**. Not missable either way. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · confidence: medium · Contradicted across sources -- see notes]

## Sealed island (P2)

Pelican Down relocks Lockdown and Connections until it's complete; the island has no FOBs and you can't fast travel off it. Tactic for the boss pair: retreat to the ship graveyard near the water to force a checkpoint -- straying too far forces a reset. Full gate list: `../nav/ow_graveyards.md`.
