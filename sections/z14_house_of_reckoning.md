## Bosses

- **Jega 'Rdomnai** -- full detail: `../npcs/jega_rdomnai.md`. [spoiler: late-game · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 0 · entity: jega_rdomnai · entity-status: hostile]

> see npcs/jega_rdomnai.md for full summary

- Jega tactics (P3): a cloaking Elite with a custom Energy Sword (PCGamesN says two), combo strings, very fast movement and a large shield and health pool. He has no ranged weapon, so distance is safety, but on high difficulties a single slash leaves you one hit from death. Turn on your flashlight to spot the cloak shimmer. Jump or grapple out of his lunges and punish during the recovery, when he cloaks and retreats. Use plasma on the shield, then kinetic; melee between combos does real damage but is risky. - **Escharum** -- full detail: `../npcs/escharum.md`. **More Than He Bargained For** -- beat him without ever fully losing your shields. Story beat: Escharum dies here, commemorated by the hidden **Legends** achievement. [spoiler: late-game (fight) / story (death) · enemy-tier: 2-3 · puzzle-tier: 0-3 · entity: escharum · entity-status: hostile · achievement: More Than He Bargained For, Legends [S]]

> see npcs/escharum.md for full summary

- Escharum tactics (P3): phase 1, he uses a Scrap Cannon variant with knockback, so stay in cover and deal kinetic damage. Phase 2, he raises an impenetrable shield: do not shoot him while it is up. Wait for the Weapon to expose a relay core, draw Escharum away from it, and destroy it. Expect a checkpoint after each relay. The outer tunnels hold explosive tanks, weapons and ammo refills. Final phase, he charges in straight lines with his hammer (Gamepressure names it the "Diminisher of Hope" and calls it an axe): grapple away, jump his slams, and shoot in bursts from range. For More Than He Bargained For, never let your shields fully break; the Steam 100% guide's cheese is Easy plus the Bandana Skull plus the Tank Gun with the Cowbell Skull.
