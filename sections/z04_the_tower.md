- Tactics (P3): he is a cloaking Elite warden with an Energy Sword and a Pulse Carbine, and he sometimes throws Plasma grenades; the arena is dark. Jump to dodge his sword lunge -- timing matters. Stick your newly acquired Threat Sensor on him to keep him revealed; upgrading it (3 cores for radius plus a second charge) helps on Heroic and Legendary. The arena is full of shock coils, and throwing them stuns him and shreds shield and health. Finish with kinetic fire (a Mangler lies on the floor). For Out with a Bang, land a Plasma grenade as the killing blow. ## Roof access and core count (web research 2026-09-28)

_source: web research 2026-09-28 (The Loadout Spartan Core page; a search summary of a roof-climb guide whose page could not be fetched; TheGamer second-region core page)_

- **The roof stays reachable after the mission; there is no point of no return** (matches `nav/z04_the_tower.md`). Reaching it is a Grappleshot climb: up one of the Tower's legs past halfway, grapple onto the small pillars, then chain grapples off the metal pieces at the roof edge. Grappleshot rank 3 (Quick Shot, 40% shorter cooldown) is recommended to chain them. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · confidence: low · Single source (search summary, page not fetched) -- verify · class:editorial-en]
- **Flying to the roof is documented:** GameRant: "The easiest way to access the top of the Tower is to simply fly to it after obtaining a Banshee or Wasp." A play report (2026-09-28) says the leg climb stalls at a lip around the roof edge, which fits the sources that require Quick Shot for the last grapple. Wasp needs 2,600 Valor (`../items/upgrades.md`); Banished vehicles like the Banshee cannot be requisitioned and must be taken in the field (`../mechanics.md`). [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 2 sources (GameRant; a second search summary) · class:editorial-en]
- **No Spartan Core is on the roof.** This corrects the first version of this section. GameRant (fetched) and Gfinity (fetched) both say the Tower has exactly 2 cores, neither on the roof: one on the ground by the gravity lift (in a small structure or building beside it) and one on the third floor in an alcove/corner as you climb the interior. The Loadout lists a third (southeast of the Tower by Banished pods) and TheGamer lists none; treat the count as 2 (matches P2) with that dispute noted. The roof collectible is the Skull, and the in-game objective waypoint points at the roof, which Gfinity calls "pointless unless you're looking for the Skull." The earlier "roof crate core" claim came from an unfetched search summary and is dropped. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · confidence: high (no roof core, 2 fetched sources agree) / medium (exact count) · Confirmed: 2 sources · class:editorial-en]

- **Where the two cores are (GamesRadar + Gfinity screenshots, fetched 2026-09-28):** Core 1 is on the ground level, in one of the open Banished storage containers under a walkway inside the complex. Enter by the front-right gate and keep bearing right past the Banished audio log; the core is a green-lit crate in one of the furthest containers on the left. Core 2 is inside the Tower via the gravity lift: past the first stairs, round the corridor to the next stairs, and on the next level up (Gfinity says third floor) a green-lit crate sits in an alcove behind containers on the right. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 2 sources · class:editorial-en]
