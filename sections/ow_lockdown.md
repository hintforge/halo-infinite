First overworld region, opened after Outpost Tremonius. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1]

## Structure notes

- Capture the first FOB (Reclaimer). A nearby Marine distress call gives **We're On Our Way**; bringing those Marines into the next mission gives **Wars with Friends** -- guides note all three (FOB, distress call, Wars with Friends) can be done together in one pass. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · achievement: Reclaimer, We're On Our Way, Wars with Friends]
- Ransom Keep is the first Banished outpost (**Outpost Discovery**). In co-op, **Controlled Demolition** requires destroying 4 Fuel Silos within 5 seconds, here or at Forge of Teash (ow_connections). [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · achievement: Outpost Discovery, Controlled Demolition]

## Enemies

- Okro 'Vagaduun (HVT, south of the starting zone) is a commonly-cited first HVT kill -- **Headhunter**. See `../items/weapons.md` for the variant-weapon unlock. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 1 · achievement: Headhunter]
