## Fast travel (P3)

Outpost Tremonius is a fast-travel destination once liberated, but it is not a lettered FOB (the FOB count stays at 12); at least one FOB must be captured before fast travel works at all. It is also where the postgame respawns you. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · confidence: high · Confirmed: 3 sources, 1 language]
