## Boss (P3)

Tremonius is fought at the end of this mission, before the elevator ride. You place a power seed in the crucible to restore power to the elevator, then deploy the Weapon at the terminal; the elevator descends carrying Tremonius and two Major Jackals with orange (hardened) shields. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: high · Confirmed: 5 sources, 1 language · entity: tremonius · entity-status: hostile]

> see npcs/tremonius.md for full summary

- Tactics: he carries a Hydra at range and a Bulldog for jetpack rushes, and has an energy shield (unusual for a Brute) that regenerates fully if you stop damaging him. Break the shield with a Pulse Carbine or overcharged Plasma Pistol, then use kinetic weapons on the head. A fusion coil can be grappled and thrown in. The Disruptor stuns him. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: high · Confirmed: 4 sources, 1 language · entity: tremonius · entity-status: hostile]

> see npcs/tremonius.md for full summary

- **Ascension** fires when he dies. **Stick Around** (kill him with the Skewer): keep the Skewer found earlier in this mission and fire it only after the shield is down. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: high · Confirmed: 4 sources, 1 language · entity: tremonius · entity-status: hostile · achievement: Ascension, Stick Around]
