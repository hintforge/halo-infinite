## Boss

- Mission boss: Adjutant Resolution -- promoted to a per-entity file at P2 (he recurs in z07, z11 and z15). [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: high · entity: adjutant_resolution · entity-status: hostile]

> see npcs/adjutant_resolution.md for full summary

- Fight 1 tactics (P3): fought mostly at range against a large Sentinel-type mech. Four glowing corner weak points; breaking one briefly stuns him, and that stun is your grenade window. Plasma and Spike grenades work well; Frag and Dynamo grenades are "ineffective". The deadly attacks are the laser beam and the rockets from the mech's lower arms, which can nearly one-shot you on Heroic and Legendary -- hug walls and cover while they fire. Up close you can land a burst, but back off before his stomp. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: high · Confirmed: 2 sources, 1 language · entity: adjutant_resolution · entity-status: hostile]

- **Known issue (co-op replay, P3):** skipping the cutscene after the boss and terminal can soft-lock players on a loading screen; the Winter Update notes list it as known. Workaround: do not skip that cutscene in co-op replays of Spire. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · confidence: high · Single source -- verify · class:official-pr]

## Collectibles

No collectible inside the Spire mission interior (correction 2026-10-11: the earlier line "1 UNSC log" counted an open-world log outside the structure). [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1]

- Location of that outside log (web research 2026-10-11): it is in the open world just outside the main Spire structure, on the ground next to a UNSC vehicle, as you approach from the northwest. MapGenie agrees: near the base of the Spire, next to a destroyed Warthog (`../optional_zones/ow_connections_collectibles.md` "Near Spire"). The walkthrough says it can still be collected after the mission ends. GGRecon's Spire collectibles guide (Dec 2021) lists two collectibles for the mission, both at that base area outside the interior: this UNSC audio log and one Spartan Core (turn right from the destroyed Warthog to a downed Pelican). No source found lists a Skull or any collectible inside the Spire interior.
