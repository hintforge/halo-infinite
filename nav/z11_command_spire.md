## Sequential gates

1. **Beam-shaft lift** -- ascend the gravity lift alongside four purple-yellow energy beams. [spoiler: late-game · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 2 · confidence: medium · Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]
2. **Sentinels first** -- kill the Aggressor Sentinels, then deploy the Weapon at the terminal. [spoiler: late-game · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · confidence: medium · Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki · entity: the_weapon · entity-status: friendly]
   > see npcs/the_weapon.md for full summary
3. **Crucible floor seal** -- retrieve a power seed and insert it into the Crucible; the central floor seal opens the lift upward. [spoiler: late-game · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 2 · confidence: medium · Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]
   - `lock:` power seed (see `architecture.md` Locks and Keys)
4. **Manufacturing window** -- Banished weapon racks, a ramp up, and a window onto a chamber where hexagonal columns are being built. [spoiler: late-game · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 2 · confidence: medium · Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]
5. **Floor portal** -- a Sangheili Ultra guards a floor portal. [spoiler: late-game · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · confidence: medium · Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]
6. **Grapple chasm** -- grapple across the chasm to the central elevator platform (there is no light bridge). [spoiler: late-game · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 2 · confidence: high · Confirmed: 2 sources, 1 language]
   - Boss pointer: pillars rise and **Adjutant Resolution** is fought a second time. Strategy not in this corpus. [spoiler: late-game · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: high · Confirmed: 2 sources, 1 language · entity: adjutant_resolution · entity-status: hostile]
   > see npcs/adjutant_resolution.md for full summary
   - `lock:` Grappleshot (from z01 -- always owned)
7. **Observation Platform** -- take the elevator up; use the terminal by the window. [spoiler: late-game · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 2 · confidence: high · Confirmed: 2 sources, 1 language]
   - Phantoms attack; a SPNKR and a Skewer are placed right beside the terminal. [spoiler: late-game · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 2 · confidence: high · Confirmed: 2 sources, 1 language]
   - `point_of_no_return:` chapter-bound
