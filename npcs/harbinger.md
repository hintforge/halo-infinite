## Combat

Three phases of Endless/Skimmer adds (including rocket-launcher Skimmers), then the Harbinger alone. Use Cindershots in the arena; melee her when she drops to lock in the phase checkpoint before firing the final shot. [spoiler: late-game · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: high · Confirmed: 3 sources]

P3 adds: she teleports, then closes in for a blade swipe -- keep moving away and damage her with the Cindershots found in the arena. After each damage threshold she shields and spawns waves of adds on both flanks; clear three waves to finish her. Windows Central and PCGamesN label the adds Banished where P1 recorded Endless/Skimmers -- a labeling difference, same encounter. (`../sections/z15_silent_auditorium.md`) [spoiler: late-game · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: high · Confirmed: 2 sources, 1 language]

## Fight detail (doctor pass 2026-10-11)

_source: web research 2026-10-11, pages opened: samurai-gamers.com/halo-infinite/the-harbinger-boss-guide/, gamerguides.com/halo-infinite/guide/bosses/main-story/how-to-defeat-the-harbinger, diamondlobby.com/halo/infinite/how-to-beat-harbinger/, gameranx.com (final boss on Legendary), gameshedge.com/halo-infinite-boss-battles-guide/ (short summary page). - **How it starts:** Gamer Guides: "A long cutscene will play here, after which you'll find yourself facing the Harbinger in combat." Diamond Lobby: "After the cutscene, she will create a yellow defense shield around her and summon the Banished." Samurai Gamers: "Start the first phase of the battle by killing a few Banished." [spoiler: story · enemy-tier: 3 · puzzle-tier: 3 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 3 sources, 1 language · class:community-guide · entity: harbinger]
- **Arena:** Gamer Guides: "a lot of space to work with in this room, but you should stick to the edges of the room as much as possible", with "enough structures above and around you to Grappleshot." Samurai Gamers: adds "appear from doors on the higher and lower platforms", Banished from "blue doors", and "set up a Drop Wall on one side of the ramp to take cover from the orbs." Gameranx: "the balcony with two Hardlight Ammo Rechargers"; the first wave "comes from the left side of the arena, and the second wave comes from the right side." [spoiler: story · enemy-tier: 3 · puzzle-tier: 3 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 3 sources, 1 language · class:community-guide · entity: harbinger]
- **Placed in the arena:** Gamer Guides: a Cindershot "in the room if you don't already have one", blast coils "located around the room", and power coils you should not waste early. Gameranx: a Sentinel Beam and an Energy Sword to grab. Samurai Gamers mentions Shock and Hard Light coils with no placement. [spoiler: story · enemy-tier: 3 · puzzle-tier: 3 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 3 sources, 1 language · class:community-guide · entity: harbinger]
- **Waves and shield cycle:** while her yellow shield is up "You won't be able to attack her" (Diamond Lobby); clear the Banished and "your 'Weapon' will say that she has intercepted her defense system"; once the bubble falls "you can attack Harbinger" (Diamond Lobby). She "will drop to her knees and become vulnerable to an attack on her health" and "her health can't be brought down more than 50% at this stage in the fight" (Gamer Guides, first phase). Wave counts differ by page: Samurai Gamers lists "two waves", then "the three waves", then "the last wave", after which "the golden barrier will dissipate, The Harbinger will be quicker and use more orbs against you"; Gamer Guides lists wave 2 with a Brute Officer, wave 3 with a heavily armored Elite Brute, then "deplete the Harbinger's shield one last time, then finish her off while she's down"; Gameranx: wave 2 has Elites, a Brute Chieftain and Skimmers. [spoiler: story · enemy-tier: 3 · puzzle-tier: 3 · confidence: medium · Contradicted across sources -- see notes · class:community-guide · entity: harbinger]
- **Damage type:** Samurai Gamers: "Shift to your Sentinel Beam weapon to deplete the shield bar", "Switch back to your Cindershot to deal damage"; "Keep using your Sentinel Beam and don't waste your explosives on the boss." Gameranx: Sentinel Beam from the upper balcony when she is far and the Energy Sword when close ("will stagger her temporarily, so she can't use her shock attack"); save the sword for "the final third of the fight." Diamond Lobby also recommends the Energy Sword and grappling "onto her every time", which makes it "harder" for her to teleport. Gamer Guides: Cindershot has "highly damaging AoE damage." [spoiler: story · enemy-tier: 3 · puzzle-tier: 3 · confidence: medium · Contradicted across sources -- see notes · class:community-guide · entity: harbinger]
- **Attacks and cornered:** Samurai Gamers: "Shoot the orb instead of evading it in order to prevent your shield to get nullified immediately"; "Jump backward when the boss got close to you. It will use its swipe attack"; "If the boss is getting close, move away and reset your position and method until the boss is finished"; "Use your Grappleshot to hang on walls or reach elevated spots in order to recover from damage." Gamer Guides: the grapple "will provide the quickest escape from a tricky situation." Gameranx warns Elites can score one-shot kills with a Stalker Rifle if you are slow. [spoiler: story · enemy-tier: 3 · puzzle-tier: 3 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 3 sources, 1 language · class:community-guide · entity: harbinger]
- **Loadout (Samurai Gamers):** Sentinel Beam, Cindershot or any weapon, fully upgraded Thruster and Grappleshot, Drop Wall. [spoiler: story · enemy-tier: 3 · puzzle-tier: 3 · confidence: medium · Single source -- verify · class:community-guide · entity: harbinger]
