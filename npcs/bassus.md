## Combat

Jump as the hammer lands, grapple away to keep your distance, strip his shield with plasma weapons, then switch to kinetic. Shock weapons and Dynamo grenades stun him, and his straight-line charges make Plasma and Spike grenades easy sticks. Explosive tanks sit behind the door on the right at the start, and three more are behind the furthest right door on the upper level. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: high · Confirmed: 4 sources, 1 language]
