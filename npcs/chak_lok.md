## Combat

Jump to dodge his sword lunge; timing matters. Stick the Threat Sensor (picked up earlier in this mission) on him to keep him revealed; upgrading it (3 cores for radius plus a second charge) helps on Heroic and Legendary. The arena is full of shock coils -- throwing them stuns him and shreds shield and health. Finish with kinetic fire (a Mangler lies on the floor). [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: high · Confirmed: 2 sources, 1 language]

Weapon racks "on the edges of the torture room" holding the Ravager ("One of the most effective"), a shotgun and others (Gamer Guides). Diamond Lobby: two kinetic ammo refills come before the top of the Tower, so save ammo for the boss. No exact rack or coil positions on any page. - **Reading him while cloaked:** Gamer Guides: he "utilizes Active Camo", and the Threat Sensor "will only work within a relatively small radius". Samurai Gamers: "Leave the zone covered by Threat Sensor and pick up a Shock Coil from corners quickly" (so pickups happen outside the sensor's reach). Diamond Lobby: "A good indicator would be if he is cloaked or not." [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 3 sources, 1 language · class:community-guide · entity: chak_lok]
- **His attacks and your answer:** Samurai Gamers: "The boss will either shoot you using a Pulse Carbine or do a jump attack using its Energy Sword." Response: "Either take cover or jump away to evade the attacks." Diamond Lobby: "The best strategy is always to keep moving." [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 2 sources, 1 language · class:community-guide · entity: chak_lok]
- **Two stages, by damage type:** (1) Shield: "use energy weapons to deplete it as fast as possible"; kinetic "will inflict damage to the shield, but nowhere near as much" (Gamer Guides); Samurai Gamers: Pulse Carbine/Rifle then "pick off the shield using the Plasma weapon you have", Disruptor as an alternative stun. Keep up pressure: "Keep up the pressure so the shields will never regenerate" (Diamond Lobby). (2) Health: "When the shield is low, you can switch to kinetic energy weapons" and, once it is down, "comparatively few hits" take his health from 100% to 0% (Gamer Guides); Samurai Gamers: "Use your Kinetic weapon and deal headshots as many as you can." No numeric thresholds on any page. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 3 sources, 1 language · class:community-guide · entity: chak_lok]
- **Shock Coils:** throw one from a distance while he has not noticed you (Samurai Gamers, Legendary); or shoot it as he nears it (Diamond Lobby). Diamond Lobby warns that throwing one during his lunge "can hurt you more than him", so keep some distance first, then hit him with the Bulldog while he is stunned or close. If he gets too close, use a melee attack of your own to briefly stun him," then "run around the room to regain some distance." Diamond Lobby: Plasma grenades "can stick to him for a huge chunk of damage." Samurai Gamers: "Rely on your grappleshot to get away from the boss" and to reach Shock Coils or weapons.
