## Current status

The campaign's first boss. He is fought at the very end of Foundation (z02), before the elevator ride that ends that mission -- not in Outpost Tremonius (z03), which shares his name. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: high · Confirmed: 5 sources, 1 language]

## Recruitment / Access

Not applicable (hostile-only encounter). You place a power seed in the crucible to restore power to the elevator, then deploy the Weapon at the terminal; the elevator descends carrying Tremonius and two Major Jackals with orange (hardened) shields. See `../nav/z02_foundation.md` gates 5-6 and `../nav/architecture.md` Zone Graph. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: high · Confirmed: 5 sources, 1 language]

## Capabilities

Hydra at range; a Bulldog for jetpack rushes; and an energy shield, which is unusual for a Brute. The shield regenerates fully if you stop damaging him. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: high · Confirmed: 4 sources, 1 language]

## Quests

Killing him lets the elevator finish its trip: riding it to the surface completes Foundation, and Outpost Tremonius (z03) begins at once. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 1 · confidence: high · Confirmed: 2 sources, 1 language]

## Combat

Break his shield with the Pulse Carbine or an overcharged Plasma Pistol, then switch to kinetic weapons to the head. A fusion coil can be grappled and thrown in. The Disruptor stuns him. One guide warns that if you die you respawn in his line of fire. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: high · Confirmed: 4 sources, 1 language]

- **Before you trigger it:** Gamer Guides: "stock up on any grenades in the area" and "make sure you have a weapon with a decent amount of ammunition in it. Ideally, you should look for a Pulse Carbine." [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Single source -- verify · class:community-guide · entity: tremonius]
- **Landmarks and cover:** Samurai Gamers: "Dance around and use the pillars or the elevator to take cover from damage especially with his charge attack." No fuller arena layout on any opened page. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Single source -- verify · class:community-guide · entity: tremonius]
- **Placed in the arena:** a Disruptor on the weapon rack "across the elevator" (Samurai Gamers: "Drop your Pulse Carbine and pick up the Disruptor on the weapon rack across the elevator"; use it only once his shield is down). Fusion coils nearby that you can grapple: "use your Grappleshot gadget to acquire a fusion coil nearby." Also "a Hard Light fusion coil obtained from Cylix room" as a backup against the Jackals. Gamer Guides says only that grenades and weapons are "in the area", with no locations. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 2 sources, 1 language · class:community-guide · entity: tremonius]
- **Jackals first:** both guides say kill the two Jackals before Tremonius. Samurai Gamers: "Setup fusion coils outside the elevator and lure the minions close to it," then set them off with a Frag Grenade or a shot. Gamer Guides: their large orange shields are tougher than blue ones, "the Pulse Carbine comes in, since it's extremely effective at destroying Jackal shields in just a few hits", and kill them fast "to eliminate the risk of being shot from behind or from the side." [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 2 sources, 1 language · class:community-guide · entity: tremonius]
- **Two stages, by damage type:** (1) shield: Pulse Carbine or a charged Plasma Pistol ("the Pulse Carbine is again the most effective weapon available to you for eating through shields"; Gamer Guides adds you cannot damage his health until the shield is gone). (2) health: Samurai Gamers: "Once the shield is gone, deal heavy damage using Sticky Grenades" and "Use your Kinetic weapon and deal headshots as many as you can." Gamer Guides: you may run out of Pulse Carbine ammo, so finish "with grenades, other weapons picked up from the area, or with your pistol." No health-percentage thresholds on either page. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 2 sources, 1 language · class:community-guide · entity: tremonius]
- **His weapons:** Gamer Guides: he "wields a CQS48 Bulldog shotgun" and "most of his shots will disperse before they hit you if you're not too close"; "keep out of melee range if at all possible, since it takes only a few hits with the shotgun to kill you." Samurai Gamers lists Bulldog, Hydra and a jetpack as his kit. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 2 sources, 1 language · class:community-guide · entity: tremonius]
- **Suggested loadout (Samurai Gamers):** Mangler or another kinetic weapon, Pulse Carbine, Disruptor as the pickup, Grappleshot. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Single source -- verify · class:community-guide · entity: tremonius]
