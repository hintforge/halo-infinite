## Combat

- **Fight 1 -- Spire (z07):** after you use the Weapon's release terminal. The arena transforms and pillars rise; use them as cover. (`../nav/z07_spire.md` gate 5) [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: high · Confirmed: 3 sources, 1 language]
- **Fight 2 -- The Command Spire (z11):** after you grapple across the chasm to the central elevator platform; pillars rise again. (`../nav/z11_command_spire.md` gate 6) [spoiler: late-game · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: high · Confirmed: 2 sources, 1 language]
- **Fight 1 tactics (P3):** fought mostly at range. Four glowing corner weak points -- breaking one briefly stuns him, and that stun is your grenade window. Plasma and Spike grenades work well; Frag and Dynamo grenades are "ineffective". The laser beam and the rockets from the mech's lower arms can nearly one-shot you on Heroic and Legendary, so hug walls and cover while they fire. Up close you can land a burst, but back off before his stomp. (`../sections/z07_spire.md`) [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: high · Confirmed: 2 sources, 1 language]
- **Fight 2 tactics (P3):** the same kit on a golden mech. The walls are irregular, so the beam is harder to break line of sight from. Sentinel drones join the fight and drop Sentinel Beams, the recommended weapon against him. Spire Stalker (40 Stalker Rifle kills) is in the same mission -- bank those kills on the adds first. (`../sections/z11_command_spire.md`) [spoiler: late-game · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: high · Confirmed: 2 sources, 1 language · achievement: Spire Stalker]

- **Start:** Gamer Guides: a cutscene at the observation-platform terminal, "at the end of which the boss fight with Adjutant Resolution begins." - **Start (lead-in narrative):** Diamond Lobby: you meet the orb, it "builds a bridge" to an elevator, and after it rises you activate your Weapon "at the end of the room", after which the Adjutant becomes a killing machine. [spoiler: story · enemy-tier: 3 · puzzle-tier: 3 · category: lore · confidence: medium · Single source -- verify · class:community-guide · entity: adjutant_resolution]
- **Arena:** a small room with four pillars: "move from one pillar to another while targeting a gun arm" and "take cover behind a pillar." Gamer Guides agrees ("nearby pillar structures", "stay behind the pillar if you get hit until your energy shield regenerates"). [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 3 sources, 1 language · class:community-guide · entity: adjutant_resolution]
- **Placed in the arena:** Gamer Guides: "a Sentinel Beam and Mangler on the nearby weapon racks", the Mangler "perhaps more useful at the beginning of the fight, and the Sentinel Beam towards the end." Samurai Gamers: ammo crates of Hard Light, Plasma and Kinetic found by the scan button, "nearby the weapon rack north of the room, close to the window." Diamond Lobby: "I came across a few lasers" in the small room. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 3 sources, 1 language · class:community-guide · entity: adjutant_resolution]
- **Phases (event-based, no health numbers on any page):** four gun arms ("Target one gun arm at a time before moving to another one"); each destroyed arm drops and stuns him ("Take advantage by switching to your Power weapon and target its core"); once all arms are gone he "will become enraged and use its Hard Light beam" and you "Go around in circles to find a spot behind the boss" to hit the core (Samurai Gamers). Gamer Guides: "You won't be able to damage Adjutant Resolution if you hit the main body of its armor"; late in the fight he "will begin firing a more powerful energy beam" to "avoid ... at all costs." Diamond Lobby: arms are "the only place you can deal damage"; in defense mode you cannot damage him, so take cover. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 3 sources, 1 language · class:community-guide · entity: adjutant_resolution]
- **Damage type:** arms: "a Kinetic or Shock weapon" (Samurai Gamers; Gamer Guides: "Any weapon can destroy the eyes"); core while stunned: Power weapon (Samurai Gamers); Gamer Guides: shoot "its core eye to inflict additional damage" after an eye is destroyed. "The boss has no shields." Grenades for extra damage (Gamer Guides, Samurai Gamers loadout), which sits against the P3 "ineffective" claim above for Frag and Dynamo; the pages name no grenade type. Loadout (Samurai Gamers): any Kinetic or Shock weapon plus grenades, any Power weapon. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 3 sources, 1 language · class:community-guide · entity: adjutant_resolution]
- **Reported from play (2026-10-11, Spire fight, one run):** one player got through it by "just shooting at the hard light rifle"; the weapon used and whether the arms were taken in order were not given, so this is one observation, not a tested tactic. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: low · Single source -- verify · class:player-report · entity: adjutant_resolution]
- **Failure note:** Samurai Gamers: "if you die from this stage, you will start from the beginning." [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Single source -- verify · class:community-guide · entity: adjutant_resolution]
