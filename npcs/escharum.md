## Combat

Two-phase fight:
- **Phase 1:** fires a knockback Scrap Cannon (a boss weapon also used by Tovarus, whose version lacks the knockback modification). After taking damage he retreats behind a shield at the room's center; destroy the glowing yellow power cell exposed in one of 4 pillars (a Plasma grenade works) three times total to break it down. **Damaging the shield also hurts Echo 216** -- see `../npcs/echo_216.md` (Echo is present and vulnerable during this phase, which is part of why he's here). Two Rocket Launchers are available on the west side of the room.
- **Phase 2:** rushes the player with a Gravity Hammer; keep moving and jump his slams.
- **Legendary tip:** the Scorpion Gun (see `../items/weapons.md`) knocks him back with every shot, and Thruster is recommended for dodging his hammer-phase slams (see `../items/builds.md`).

- **Shield phase:** GGRecon: "After destroying one of the power cells, the shield will lower briefly"; "prioritise doing the minimum required damage, so he goes to the shields." Samurai Gamers: "Do not attack the shield"; destroy an orange relay with the Battle Rifle to make him vulnerable for a short period; Rocket Launcher "can destroy one power relay quickly"; throw Shock coils or grenades first to slow him. - **Shield phase (checkpoint note naming Echo 216):** Samurai Gamers: "Hit a checkpoint after Echo 216 is confirmed safe by The Weapon." [spoiler: story · enemy-tier: 3 · puzzle-tier: 3 · category: lore · confidence: medium · Single source -- verify · class:community-guide · entity: escharum]
- **First phase movement:** "The boss is slow due to Scrap Cannon so move around the corners using Thrusters" (Samurai Gamers); GGRecon: kite him "around the more narrow areas to make the most of his lack of mobility." Gamer Guides: don't stand still long except when shooting relays. [spoiler: late-game · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 3 sources, 1 language · class:community-guide · entity: escharum]
- **Hammer phase:** Gamer Guides: he "will now wield his melee weapon" and moves faster; use blast coils to cut his health quickly, and his melee does "massive damage." GGRecon: stay mobile with Thruster and Grappleshot; "Avoid Fusion Coils, so the Gravity Hammer splash damage doesn't blow them up near you." Samurai Gamers: "Doing a normal dodge is ineffective so use a Thruster to move left or right." Grapple use: "to create some distance between yourself and Escharum to escape melee range" (Gamer Guides); Samurai Gamers adds a ceiling anchor: "attach it to the ceiling in order to transfer to another side of the room", and "Drag the boss outside and into the hallway." [spoiler: late-game · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 3 sources, 1 language · class:community-guide · entity: escharum]
- **Damage type:** GGRecon: "a combination of plasma and kinetic weaponry" (Shock Rifle recommended for damage output). Samurai Gamers: Battle Rifle on relays; "Disruptor can deal shock damage over time while the Mangler can stagger the boss if you deal a headshot"; Needler or Pulse Rifle as alternatives to grenades; Plasma or Spike grenades. Loadout (Samurai Gamers): Battle Rifle, Needler, Disruptor, Mangler, fully upgraded Thruster and Grappleshot. [spoiler: late-game · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 2 sources, 1 language · class:community-guide · entity: escharum]
