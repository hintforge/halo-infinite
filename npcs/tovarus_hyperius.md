## Combat

- **Kill order is [Contradicted across sources].** Five guides (Windows Central, ScreenRant, GamerTweak, The Nerd Stash, Samurai Gamers) say kill Hyperius first: lure him to the west side, skewer or rocket the Chopper, and drain his shield with a Pulse Carbine or Sentinel Beam. PCGamesN alone says rocket Tovarus first from the destroyed ship, then use his dropped Scrap Cannon on Hyperius. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Contradicted across sources -- see notes]
- **Rules that matter:** the bosses' shields regenerate unless you keep damage on them. Leaving the arena resets the damage on the brothers but not the damage on the Chopper (GamerTweak). Reinforcements arrive after the first kill. Retreating to the ship graveyard near the water forces a checkpoint; straying too far forces a reset (Samurai Gamers). There is no FOB resupply on the island. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Single source -- verify · class:editorial-en (arena reset, checkpoint leash)]

## Fight detail (doctor pass 2026-10-11)

- **How it starts:** Gamer Guides: the fight "will begin as soon as you reach their position in the marshy land beneath the AA guns that you've just destroyed", and "Hyperius will begin the fight in a modified Brute Chopper." Samurai Gamers' walkthrough: "Head back to the Pelican and prepare to battle Tovarus and Hyperius." [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 2 sources, 1 language · class:community-guide · entity: tovarus_hyperius]
- **Landmarks:** rocks and treelines for cover (Samurai Gamers); "Tovarus ... will be moving between the platforms in the middle of the area" and you can go "up the wrecked buildings surrounding the platforms in the middle or the hill to the south" (Samurai Gamers walkthrough); Tovarus enters "the broken ship located on the east" and later appears "on a platform located on the west side" (Samurai Gamers boss page); Gamer Guides: "the ruins of one of the nearby ships". GamesHedge: "plenty of shipwrecks", a Warthog with a gunner turret, and explosive barrels. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 3 sources, 1 language · class:community-guide · entity: tovarus_hyperius]
- **Placed in the arena:** a SPNKr Rocket Launcher on the ridge to the northeast (Samurai Gamers, both pages); a Rocket Launcher and a Hydra in a room Tovarus enters (Samurai Gamers); Ravagers on weapon racks in the marsh and a Skewer "on the nearby rocks (use AI Scan to locate it)" (Gamer Guides); weapons dropped by dead Brutes (Samurai Gamers); a Spartan core on the wreckage southeast of the middle platforms (Samurai Gamers walkthrough). [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 3 sources, 1 language · class:community-guide · entity: tovarus_hyperius]
- **Hyperius:** both Samurai Gamers pages and Gamer Guides start with him. Take the Chopper down "by lobbing fusion coils first" (Samurai Gamers walkthrough) -- or "making removing Hyperius from his vehicle your primary goal" (Gamer Guides), and "If you're able to destroy the vehicle while he's close to it, he'll suffer from the explosive damage too." On foot he "will attempt to get near you to take you out with his Ravager Blade so pull away from him while destroying his shield." Shield: "Drain the shield ... using Plasma weapons", then "switch to a Power weapon to finish off the boss" and aim for the head. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 3 sources, 1 language · class:community-guide · entity: tovarus_hyperius]
- **Tovarus:** Gamer Guides: after Hyperius Tovarus "will begin to actively pursue you", has "a kind of jet pack which allows him to fly through the air while shooting at you" and is harder to hit airborne; "focus fire while he's in a confined space"; the Ravager "will inflict massive damage to both." Samurai Gamers: "Drain the shield ... from a distance using a Sentinel Beam or any Plasma weapon," then "use a Sniper Rifle or Skewer to damage the boss from a distance"; "If the boss is still alive, drag the boss inside again and pick up other weapons." [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 2 sources, 1 language · class:community-guide · entity: tovarus_hyperius]
- **Tovarus (kinship note):** Gamer Guides wording: "his brother Tovarus". [spoiler: story · enemy-tier: 3 · puzzle-tier: 3 · category: lore · confidence: medium · Single source -- verify · class:community-guide · entity: tovarus_hyperius]
- **Cornered or low on health:** Samurai Gamers: "Get behind cover or use the Drop Wall when he fires his Scrap Cannon"; "Feel free between the wrecked buildings if Tovarus gets out of view"; if low on health, "especially in Legendary mode", go to the southwest edge of the area; "Don't go too far or else it will force a reset"; "Stay around the ship graveyard near the water and it can force a checkpoint for you." Gamer Guides: "find a rock / structure to hide behind", and the Grappleshot to "quickly escape his fire if you're exposed". [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Confirmed: 2 sources, 1 language · class:community-guide · entity: tovarus_hyperius]
- **Loadouts (Samurai Gamers):** Hyperius: Pulse Carbine, Battle Rifle or any Power weapon, upgraded Grappleshot and Thruster (two charges). Tovarus: Sniper Rifle or Skewer from a Brute drop, grenades, Sentinel Beam or any Plasma weapon, upgraded Grappleshot and Drop Wall. [spoiler: progression · enemy-tier: 1 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium · Single source -- verify · class:community-guide · entity: tovarus_hyperius]
