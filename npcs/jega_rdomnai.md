## Capabilities

A cloaking Elite with a custom Energy Sword (PCGamesN says two), combo strings, very fast movement and a large shield and health pool. He has no ranged weapon, so distance is safety -- but on high difficulties a single slash leaves you one hit from death. ## Combat

Keep Threat Sensors active on him at all times. When he vanishes (his signature ability), stand over a floor hologram and jump back and forth while turning until he reappears. **Known soft-lock:** missing the floor hologram that spawns him can lock the encounter. LASO guides rely on constant Threat Sensor coverage for this fight (see `../items/builds.md` "Where guides disagree"). [spoiler: late-game · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: medium (2 sources)]

P3 adds: turn on your flashlight to spot his cloak shimmer. Jump or grapple out of his lunges and punish during the recovery, when he cloaks and retreats. Use plasma on the shield, then kinetic. Melee between combos does real damage but is risky. (`../sections/z14_house_of_reckoning.md`) [spoiler: late-game · enemy-tier: 2 · puzzle-tier: 0 · confidence: high · Confirmed: 2 sources, 1 language]
