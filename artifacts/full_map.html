Halo Infinite collectible map

Real map, whole-map grid A-H (west to east) by 1-9 (north to south). Each item has a code: letter = type, number = reading order. Green ring and tick = taken. Red round H = weapon you keep for good after killing a High Value Target; grey square W = loose weapon lying in the world (unlocks nothing). Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan. Click a dot to see what it is; mark it taken in the side panel (Mark taken button or the list tick boxes).
